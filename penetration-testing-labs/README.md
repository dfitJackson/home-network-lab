## Lab Projects

The [Lab Projects index](./lab-projects/README.md) contains controlled, non-production exercises used to practise reconnaissance, enumeration, exploitation, validation, and documentation.

These exercises are clearly identified as lab work and do not represent testing against live client systems.

## Real-World Experience

The [Real-World Experience index](./real-world-experience/README.md) contains sanitized summaries of authorized professional work.

To protect client confidentiality:

- Client names, URLs, credentials, and proprietary data are excluded.
- Raw evidence is not published.
- Findings are described only at the level needed to demonstrate methodology, validation, and reporting experience.

## Methodology and Ethics

Across both types of work, the approach emphasizes:

- Defined authorization and scope
- Manual validation supported by appropriate tools
- Reproducible testing and false-positive elimination
- Clear separation between lab exercises and professional engagements
- Sanitized evidence and responsible disclosure

Additional projects will be added only after the underlying work and its final outcome have been documented.
